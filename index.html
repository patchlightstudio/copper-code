Why this matters

Technicians use a shared color sequence to identify copper pairs, follow cable counts, and terminate or troubleshoot the correct conductors. A wrong pair can mean a wrong connection.

How to calculate a pair

Subtract full groups of 25 to get a position from 1 to 25. Within that position, groups of five determine the first color. The position within each five determines the second.

Example: 40 − 25 = 15 → Black / Slate.

What is a binder group?

In many multi-pair telephone cables, 25-pair groups are marked with colored binder tapes. Binder identification and cable count conventions vary. Confirm the cable's actual labeling before making connections.
